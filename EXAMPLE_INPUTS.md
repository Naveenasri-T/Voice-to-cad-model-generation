## 🎨 WHAT YOU'LL GET IN EVERY DRAWING

### ✅ Multiple Views (MANDATORY):
- **TOP VIEW** - Floor plan from above
- **FRONT VIEW** - Primary elevation
- **SIDE/PROJECTION VIEW** - Profile/section

### ✅ Dimensions (ALL IN MM):
- Overall dimensions (5000 mm, 3500 mm, etc.)
- Room dimensions
- Door/window sizes
- Furniture dimensions
- Critical clearances

### ✅ Grid Reference System:
```
    A    B    C    D    E    F
  +----+----+----+----+----+----+
1 |                             |
  +                             +
2 |    [Building Layout]        |
  +                             +
3 |                             |
  +----+----+----+----+----+----+
```

### ✅ Title Block (Bottom Right):
```
Drawing Name: [Your Project]
Scale: 1:100
Date: [Auto-generated]
Material: [As specified]
Sheet: A4
```

### ✅ Labels & Annotations:
- Room names (LIVING ROOM, BEDROOM, etc.)
- Component labels (Door, Window, Wardrobe, etc.)
- Material specifications (18mm Plywood, MDF, Glass)
- Installation notes

### ✅ Line Hierarchy:
- **Heavy lines (3.0)** - Exterior walls, perimeters
- **Medium lines (2.0)** - Interior walls, major elements
- **Thin lines (1.0)** - Furniture, fixtures
- **Very thin (0.5)** - Dimensions, grid, hatching

---

## ❌ WHAT YOU WON'T GET ANYMORE

❌ Simple 3D boxes floating in space  
❌ Single view only  
❌ Missing dimensions  
❌ No labels or annotations  
❌ Empty rectangles  
❌ Incomplete drawings

## 💡 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### 1. **Be Specific About Requirements:**
✅ Good: "2BHK apartment with open kitchen, attached bathrooms"  
❌ Vague: "Make a house"

### 2. **Mention Interior Details:**
✅ Good: "Wardrobe with 3 shelves, hanging rod, and 2 drawers"  
❌ Basic: "A wardrobe"

### 3. **Include Functional Spaces:**
✅ Good: "Office with 6 workstations, conference room, and pantry"  
❌ Incomplete: "An office"

### 4. **Specify Relationships:**
✅ Good: "Master bedroom with attached bathroom and walk-in closet"  
❌ Unclear: "Bedroom and bathroom"
